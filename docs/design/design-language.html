Sofos design language

Same trail-map idea, recolored to your blue. Navy stays the brand and does the primary work; a single bright azure is spent only on where you are now. Destructive actions stay red because red is what "delete" means everywhere. No green, no orange.

Palette — all blue, one accent

The trick that makes it feel designed rather than monochrome: navy is everywhere, so it stops being special — the azure is the only thing on any screen that isn't navy or gray, so your eye goes straight to "you are here." That's the whole point of holding one accent in reserve.
