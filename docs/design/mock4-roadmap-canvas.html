Roadmap as a draggable trail canvas

The old roadmap drew each waypoint's line as a fixed stub — when cards wrapped to a second row the stubs pointed at nothing and the trail visually broke. Here the trail is one continuous line drawn marker-to-marker, wherever the cards are: solid behind you, dashed ahead, azure = you are here.

▶ TRY IT: grab any card and drag it around — the trail follows. This is the actual proposed behavior.
