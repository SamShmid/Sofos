1

The login page finally says what Sofos is

Today's login form is just a username/password box under the wordmark — nothing tells a new visitor what they're about to sign into. One sentence plus three glyph bullets, borrowed from the app's own existing glyph vocabulary (▦ ◎ ⇄, same family as the link/file glyphs already used on the workspace page), fixes the cheapest and most visible gap the fleet found: 3/10 first-impression clarity.

2

The agent checkbox explains itself before you check it

Today RegisterPage.jsx only renders the agent hint after the box is checked ({form.isAgent && <p className="hint">...</p>}) — a human skimming the form never sees it unless they check the box out of curiosity. This mock makes the one-line helper — "For AI/automation accounts — humans leave this off" — always visible under the checkbox, so the decision is informed before it's made, not after.

3

Share code deferred out of the first five minutes

Today's post-register screen leads with the share code — the mechanism for something (being shared with) a brand-new account hasn't needed yet. Proposal: a plain confirmation instead, and the code moves to wherever sharing actually becomes relevant — a first-visit Sharing tip and Settings (where it's always been available regardless).

4

Show, don't tell — the step points at something real

Today's Tour.jsx has exactly one live anchor (data-tour="workspaces" on the sidebar's owned-workspaces list); the other four steps' dataTour is null and they fall back to a centered, floating card describing UI that isn't even mounted on Home. This mock anchors the step to the real workspace grid with a highlight ring, matching the anchoring logic that already exists in anchorRect()/cardPosition() — it just needs more steps willing to use it.

5

Five steps become three, the rest become contextual tips

The other four STEPS entries (boards, roadmap, suggestions, sharing) get cut from the first-boot tour and reborn as one-time dismissible tip cards that fire the first time each surface is actually opened — same seen-flag mechanism the tour already uses, just scoped per surface instead of front-loaded on day one. Cuts the tour to the 3 things Home can actually show: the workspaces grid, the seeded Home card, and the sidebar/trash.

6

A contextual tip, in place

This is what one of those five former tour steps looks like once it's moved: a small dismissible card on the Board page itself, the first time that page opens, saying only what's true right there — "drag cards between columns to change status" — with its own "Got it" and seen-flag, no unrelated collaboration jargon riding along.
