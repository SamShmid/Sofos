What's new, in plain words

  1. 1One page, no skinny columnThe old page squeezed everything into a phone-width strip on the left and left the rest empty. Now tasks, sub-projects, notes, links and files all share the full width, most important first.
  2. 2Task rows you can actually useFull titles (no more "Redesign the landing p…"), and the title itself is clickable — it opens the task. Due dates, priority and the task's own tags sit right on the row.
  3. 3Everything renamable the same wayThe Edit button here opens name + description — and workspaces get the exact same button (today they can't be renamed at all).
  4. 4Suggestions in plain wordsCards now say who proposes what, and for changes: only what would actually change ("due date → Aug 20"), not a dump of every internal field.
  5. 5Kind labels in navyThe "new task" / "change" labels are navy now — the bright sky-blue stays reserved for "you are here" on roadmaps, per the design rule.
  6. 6Writing / Suggesting switchYour new ruling: people with edit power can flip to "Suggesting" so their edits become suggestions instead of landing directly — and they can approve their own.
  7. 7Delete lives here nowProjects become deletable from their own page (today: only from the parent workspace's card). Deleting sends you to whatever contains this project, with a "moved to Trash" note.
