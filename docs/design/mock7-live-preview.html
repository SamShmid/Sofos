What this depicts

  1. 1No more render-vs-edit statev1's click-a-line-to-flip-to-raw interaction is gone entirely, per Samuel's ruling ("I don't want render vs. editing state — it's just edit and we render as the user is typing"). Every line in the note body below is always rendered. There's no raw mode to click into, no line that goes monospace, no mode switch.
  2. 2Type in the sandbox to see it render liveThe dashed box at the bottom of the note surface is a real editable line. Type # , **bold**, *italic*, `code`, or - and watch it convert the instant the characters land — no separate preview step, no toggle.
  3. 3The open question — reveal on cursor, or never?The toggle above the note body is the one thing Samuel is deciding. Mode A (⦿, default) mirrors real Obsidian: click into a rendered construct — a bold word, a heading, a code span, a link, a quote, a list marker — and just that construct's syntax characters reveal inline, dimmed, in place. Click elsewhere and they tuck away; nothing else on the line changes. Mode B (○) is Typora/Google-Docs style: the same click never reveals characters — formatting would be edited via shortcuts or a toolbar instead of typing raw syntax.
  4. 4Comment marks unchanged (P55)"July 30 freeze" still carries the gold dotted-underline comment highlight — untouched by the toggle above, since it's not a markdown construct.
  5. 5Suggestion marks unchanged (P57)"No exceptions." still carries the red strikethrough .hunk-live treatment — it's the target of Dana's pending suggestion (see the margin card), not markdown strikethrough syntax.
  6. 6Known limitation of the sandboxIt re-renders the whole line on every keystroke and re-places the caret at the end — smooth for typing forward, but arrow-keying back into the middle of the line and inserting a character will bump the caret to the end. This is a feel-demo for live rendering, not a real editor.
  7. 7Everything else is mock6Note-switcher sidebar, breadcrumbs, meta line, margin cards for suggestions/comments — unchanged. This mock is scoped to the editing surface only.
